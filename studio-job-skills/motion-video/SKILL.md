# Motion video

One job, the same one as the "Motion video" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Make a video of the assembly moving through its range of motion.

The assembly is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: An MP4 video of the assembly moving through its range of motion.
