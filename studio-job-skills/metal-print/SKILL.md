# Check for metal printing

One job, the same one as the "Check for metal printing" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part for metal printing: overhangs and supports, thin walls, small holes and bridges, and the best orientation. Tell me what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Findings for metal printing, each passing, to fix or to check, with the fix.
