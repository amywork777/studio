# Fit and clearance

One job, the same one as the "Fit and clearance" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the parts in the assembly for collisions and tight clearances, and tell me where they touch or rub and by how much.

The assembly is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A report of any collision, where the parts touch and the tightest gaps.
