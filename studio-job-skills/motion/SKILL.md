# Range of motion

One job, the same one as the "Range of motion" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Move the assembly through its range of motion and tell me where parts collide, bind or run out of travel.

The assembly is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A report of the closest gap and any collision at each step of its travel.
