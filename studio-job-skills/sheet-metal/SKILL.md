# Check as sheet metal

One job, the same one as the "Check as sheet metal" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part as a sheet metal part: bend radii, reliefs, and hole and edge distances. Tell me what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Findings for sheet metal, each passing, to fix or to check, with the fix.
