# Check for resin printing

One job, the same one as the "Check for resin printing" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part for resin printing: supports, thin walls, small holes and fine features, and the best orientation. Tell me what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Findings for resin printing, each passing, to fix or to check, with the fix.
