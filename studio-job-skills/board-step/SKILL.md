# Board STEP

One job, the same one as the "Board STEP" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `pcb` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Export the board as a STEP file with its parts, to design an enclosure around.

The board is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: The board with its parts as a STEP file, to design its enclosure around. Downloads its board tools on first use, about 100 MB.
