# Export

One job, the same one as the "Export" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Export the part as STEP, STL and 3MF.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: STEP, STL and 3MF files, saved beside the part.
