# Exploded view

One job, the same one as the "Exploded view" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Make exploded-view pictures of the assembly that show how its parts go together.

The assembly is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Exploded-view PNGs that show how its parts go together.
