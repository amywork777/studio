# Weight and center of mass

One job, the same one as the "Weight and center of mass" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Tell me the weight, volume and center of mass of the part in the project's material, and what makes up most of the weight.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: The weight, volume and center of mass in the project's material, and what weighs most.
