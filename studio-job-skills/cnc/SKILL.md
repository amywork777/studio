# Check for CNC machining

One job, the same one as the "Check for CNC machining" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part for CNC machining: tool access, internal corners, thin walls and setups. Tell me what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Findings for CNC machining, each passing, to fix or to check, with the fix.
