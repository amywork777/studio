# Check for injection molding

One job, the same one as the "Check for injection molding" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Check the part for injection molding: draft, wall thickness and undercuts. Tell me what to change.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Findings for injection molding, each passing, to fix or to check, with the fix.
