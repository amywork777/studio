# Pictures from every side

One job, the same one as the "Pictures from every side" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Make pictures of the part from the front, side, top and an angle, for a presentation.

The part is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: Four PNGs, from the front, side, top and an angle, for a presentation.
