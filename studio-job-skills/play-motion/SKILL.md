# Motion to play

One job, the same one as the "Motion to play" tile in a part's Toolbox in Studio. Do it with
the `cad` skill: read that skill first and follow it, then do what the person asks:

> Add a motion to the assembly that takes it through its range of motion, for the viewer's Motion to play. If it has one already, tell me its name and how to play it.

The assembly is the one the person names, else the one open beside the chat.
Ask which when neither says.

What they get: A motion of the assembly through its range of travel, which the viewer's Motion plays.
